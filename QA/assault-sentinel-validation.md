# Assault Sentinel validation

Basic tracked enemy after the Assault Sentinel concept sheet. Files: `dist/assault-sentinel-config.js` (all tuning), `dist/assault-sentinel-model.js` (procedural low-poly model, ~2.6k triangles, ~3 m), `dist/assault-sentinel.js` (`AssaultSentinel extends EvacRaider`, plus the shared `SentinelFX` pool).

- Reuses EvacRaider target selection/alerts, `fireBolt` projectiles (trace, hero hits, Rift redirect, squad energy trails), `breakApart` debris and wrecks, and the `UnitDamage.modifyDamage` hook for armour. No hero weapon code changed.
- Armour (by hit shape and impact direction from the torso facing): front plates ×0.3, frontal hull ×0.35, sides ×0.85, rear hull ×1.4, rear reactor core ×1.75. While the chest is open (firing, venting, slam recovery) a front core shape appears, any core hit is ×2.4 and frontal hits ×0.85. Armour hits show sparks and a metallic ping instead of the red damage flash; core hits flare and thump.
- Attacks: 0.8 s telegraphed charge (glowing muzzles, warning ring) then a 4-bolt burst (11 damage, 34 m/s, mixed direct/lead); slam when a hero is within 5.5 m — 0.95 s raised wind-up with a filling ground ring, 6.2 m shockwave for 22 damage plus knockback, 1.6 s exposed recovery. Stun cancels attacks. Death: 0.55 s core overload, explosion via the existing robot death effects, debris; hit shapes are removed immediately so it cannot be hit or attack again.
- City Liberation: Sentinels were later removed from City Liberation at the owner's request. They remain in Combat Testing; `QA/assault-sentinel-test.mjs` asserts that the district packs use only Bruisers and drones.
- Combat Testing presets: `sentinel-1`, `sentinel-3` (`?scene=testing&enemy=sentinel-1`).

Tests
- `node QA/assault-sentinel-test.mjs`: model parts/budget/scale and independent treads; every armour zone and exposure multiplier; Kage dash flash; full burst → vent → slam cycle with telegraphs and knockback; strafing dodges bolts; protected heroes not slammed; stun; overload death, single explosion, debris, no attacks after death, reset; three Sentinels keep apart and all fire; detours around a wall; City Liberation pool, BS pack spawn, scaling, sound cue, kill reward, reset (needs the private SpiderBot asset).
- Browser (cyberpunk city): CPU Volt kills one in ~17.5 s (plates and exposed core hits), CPU Kage in ~16 s (frontal hits mostly blocked by armour); Volt vs three Sentinels. City Liberation with Luxmancer: real `spawnPack` spawn at the map edge, navigation into range, bursts hit the hero; Luxmancer lance deals 123 into the armoured front, 351×2.4 into the open front core and ×1.75 into the rear core; kill pays the Sentinel reward. A 150 s natural City Liberation run spawns a Sentinel from the pack rotation with no page errors.
