# Building damage smoke — revision 148

Buildings begin emitting sustained smoke at 20% overall structural damage. Damage is weighted by section maximum health; detached sections count as lost structure. Minor isolated panel damage remains smoke-free. Emission increases from 4 to 10 puffs/second per building, reaching maximum at 65% damage. Up to three separated damaged sections supply plumes. Intact structures vent just outside the nearest facade with outward drift so walls do not hide the effect. Sources follow current section transforms, so collapsed buildings smoke from rubble rather than their former storeys.

`dist/building-smoke.js` exports the thresholds, rates, .25-second evaluation interval, three-source limit, and global 24-puff/second cap. `battle.js` renders 3.5–4.5-second expanding/rising puffs through the existing 180-instance smoke pool, with a final size fade. No extra meshes, materials, dependencies, collision, or gameplay damage. Rebuild resets emission credit, sources, counters, and visible particles.

Passed `QA/building-smoke-test.mjs`: damage threshold/severity, live rubble source positions, 31-building global budget, rebuild reset. Passed `QA/building-smoke-browser.cjs`: desktop and touch arena, actual damage impacts, clean vs smoking building, upward plumes at multiple heights, at most one added draw call for the existing pool, smoke after physical collapse, full rebuild, no page errors. Screenshots: `/tmp/building148-smoke-{desktop,phone}.png`. Existing 12-storey collapse regression also passes. Physical phone frame rate is unmeasured.

## Revision 155 — visible smoke from concentrated damage

Smoke now also starts when four sections are at least 65% damaged/detached, even if the whole-building damage is below 20%. This fixes tall towers with a badly blasted local area remaining smoke-free. Overall damage still provides the original trigger, and severity increases emission. Darker .46-opacity plumes are larger and last 5–6 seconds. A dedicated 160-instance building-smoke pool keeps their rendering independent of ordinary combat smoke; the global 24-puff/second budget remains. Rebuild clears both pools.

Node tests cover local damage on a 200-section tower as well as thresholds, severity, source positions, global budget and reset. Desktop/touch browser checks now damage only four panels on floor 6 of a real 12-storey arena building, verify sustained smoke, one extra instanced draw call, settled-rubble smoke and reset, with no page errors. Visually reviewed `/tmp/building155-smoke-desktop.png`; phone capture also saved. Physical phone performance remains unmeasured.
