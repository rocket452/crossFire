# Building damage smoke — revision 148

Buildings begin emitting sustained smoke at 20% overall structural damage. Damage is weighted by section maximum health; detached sections count as lost structure. Minor isolated panel damage remains smoke-free. Emission increases from 4 to 10 puffs/second per building, reaching maximum at 65% damage. Up to three separated damaged sections supply plumes. Intact structures vent just outside the nearest facade with outward drift so walls do not hide the effect. Sources follow current section transforms, so collapsed buildings smoke from rubble rather than their former storeys.

`dist/building-smoke.js` exports the thresholds, rates, .25-second evaluation interval, three-source limit, and global 24-puff/second cap. `battle.js` renders 3.5–4.5-second expanding/rising puffs through the existing 180-instance smoke pool, with a final size fade. No extra meshes, materials, dependencies, collision, or gameplay damage. Rebuild resets emission credit, sources, counters, and visible particles.
