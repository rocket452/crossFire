# Volt Rush lightning-wave rescue dash — revision 62

Manual rescue dash is now an electric transformation, using the existing ability and ally movement adapters.

- Volt's actual character root disappears while a curved cyan-white electric wave travels. Nearby soldiers/tanks/civilians disappear into it; continuous swept contact collects additional allies farther along the route. Real weighted infantry meshes and their instanced weapons are hidden, as are tank roots and civilian instances. Conversion and reformation rings/arcs are pooled, with 32 simultaneous pulses and the existing 500-segment electric render cap.
- The wave uses Volt's small collision footprint. A wide carried tank or an ally ahead no longer prevents an otherwise clear manual dash. Intact walls, major obstacles and unsupported rooftop edges still stop the wave. Low-rubble support and descending small support edges are handled without falsely blocking the dash.
- All passengers reform in safe non-overlapping spaces near the endpoint. Original offsets are preferred, then nearby clear positions; the original safe position is a last-resort fallback if the destination is completely obstructed. Health is preserved, direct/blast damage and collision are suppressed only while converted, and hostile missile locks are broken on conversion. Collider history is rebased on reformation so a missile cannot hit an invisible swept path.
- Infantry/tank AI and civilian formation/boarding wait while phased. Civilians retain the existing carried-group route handoff and resume their street routes after reforming. Rift portals cannot remove an ally from an active wave. CPU rescue selection and existing real-impact rescue-credit rules are reused.
- Right-click, E and mobile DASH remain the inputs. Manual movement input chooses dash direction; without movement it follows the view. A directional dodge does not snap the player's camera. A 0.2-second input buffer catches landing transitions. Failed attempts show surface/cooldown/obstruction feedback and do not consume cooldown. Air/wall dashes are not added; use a street, terrain or roof surface.

Configurable `DASH` values in `dist/runner-dash.js`: range 10 m, initial pickup radius 5 m, wave contact radius 2.4 m plus unit collider extent, speed 36 m/s (about 0.28 s transit), cooldown 5 s, input buffer 0.2 s. No charge cost. Primary attacks/stun do not fire during transit. Cancel, exit and rebuild release every phased ally.
