# Civilian evacuation scene — revision 46

The Scene menu keeps City battle as the default and adds Civilian evacuation via `?scene=evacuation`. Changing scenes reloads into a fresh isolated simulation. Both reuse the original city, armies, heroes, five-second robot opening delay and beam → missiles → plasma rotation.

Two modeled subway entrances occupy opposite corners (-48,-48) and (48,48). Eight civilians emerge immediately, then every 30 simulation seconds from alternating entrances. Their waypoint routes follow the outer streets and existing ±24m avenues to the landing zone at (0,48). Pausing freezes spawning, movement and flight. Active civilians cap at 64 and render in one instanced draw with running limbs. Outer streets connect the entrances to the city grid.

The low-poly tandem-rotor Chinook has 600 HP and 16 seats. It departs with eight passengers, or after a 12-second boarding gap with a partial load. After an eight-second departure the passengers count as safe; the helicopter returns after ten seconds. Deaths count as losses. Destroying the helicopter loses its passengers, stops new waves, and displays a rebuild prompt. Rebuild clears civilians, flight state, deaths and rescue counts. A landing-zone camera button and scene status strip expose progress.

Evacuation weapons use visible heroes, civilians and the Chinook. The selector cycles categories, skips empty/occluded categories, and falls back to visible civilians/helicopter when heroes are hidden. Building and terrain traces gate target acquisition. Beam wind-up retains its existing target lock; moving missile targets retain stable references. Plasma still marks a ground impact zone under the chosen target. The existing heroes remain invulnerable as in the base prototype; civilians and the Chinook take real weapon damage. Solara can also cover threatened civilians with her domes.
