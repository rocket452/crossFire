# Through-cover actors — revision 99

`actor-silhouettes.js` shares existing enemy mesh geometry and civilian instance matrices. Siege Walker and drone silhouettes are dark red (#791d26); civilian cohorts are dark yellow (#8c730c). A depth-greater overlay and reserved stencil bit 2 show occluded fragments while suppressing visible actor faces. Opaque actors render after world blockers so visible-fragment stencil tests use the completed building depth. The civilian crowd uses one additional instanced draw, retaining existing poses, grouping, boarding, death and rescue-phase filtering. Dead robots and detached wreckage lose their silhouette.

Civilian cohorts flash together between ochre and red at 3Hz when the existing minimap threat detector marks beam charging/firing, missiles, impending plasma or drone fire. A civilian damage callback also keeps its cohort flashing for 0.9 simulation seconds. Expired danger returns to dark yellow. These are visual cues only; no targeting, damage, navigation or visibility rules are changed. The effect works in observer and hero cameras.

`QA/actor-silhouettes-browser.cjs` uses the real renderer/units in the evacuation scene, with a controlled opaque blocker. On desktop and emulated phone it reads framebuffer pixels to verify red enemies and yellow civilians behind cover, exercises synchronized warning colors, recent damage and expiry, and verifies dead/removed actors disappear. Screenshots: `/tmp/actor99-desktop.png`, `/tmp/actor99-phone.png`. `QA/civilian-targeting-test.mjs` verifies existing threat detection remains correct. Physical-phone FPS/thermal performance has not been measured.
