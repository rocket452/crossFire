# Pyroclast validation

Pyroclast is the flying flame supervillain in Combat Testing (Test setup → Enemy preset → "Pyroclast — flying flame supervillain", or `?scene=testing&enemy=pyroclast`). Model, fire and effects are procedural (`dist/pyroclast-model.js`, `dist/pyroclast-fx.js`); no licensed assets.

- `node QA/pyroclast-test.mjs`: one 14,000 HP boss unit with head/torso/leg shapes (head ×1.5); full fireball → tornado → meteor cycle at 30/60/120 fps; stays airborne; three giant moving flame tornadoes (4.6 m radius, 24 m tall) rise in a telegraphed ring around the hero and lift them; 16 meteors plus a giant finale on the target, falling out of a burning sky rift; each has a warning ring with a converging lock-on ring and a target beam that must finish its telegraph before the rock falls; impacts throw molten debris, roll a dust ring and leave crater fires, and the rift closes after the shower or if Pyroclast falls; 60 m/s fireballs home gently, fuse on near misses and drop after overshooting; protected heroes take no damage; stun pauses attacks; death falls, explodes, reports defeat and respawns cleanly.
- Browser smoke (`?scene=testing` via the preset UI and `?scene=city-test&enemy=pyroclast&hero=volt`): PYROCLAST boss bar visible and falling, all three attacks fire, no page errors.
- Model (`dist/pyroclast-model.js`): follows the Pyroclast character sheet — near-black faceted body with orange V seams and a two-tone flame emblem, lilac-grey armour only on shoulders, bracers, belt (orange chevron), diamond knee guards and boots (orange gems and glowing soles), black kite mask with large slanted eyes and ear rings under a cel-shaded flame head with drifting flecks, red V collar, red front coat flaps and a knee-length cape whose hems burn with animated cel flame teeth; cape emblem rides the cloth displacement. Static parts merged per pivot (~74 meshes, ~10k triangles).
- Rig (`dist/pyroclast-rig.js`): spring-driven poses — hover with breathing, head tracking and speed-trailing legs; alternating-hand palm blasts (R/L/R) with cock, thrust and recoil; tornado arm-sweep and aerial slam; meteor sky call then conductor gestures; stun slump, damage flinch, death flail; cape and coat tails stream with speed. Covered by the rig block in `QA/pyroclast-test.mjs`.
- Visuals were iterated in the cyberpunk city (fire crown, burning cape, boot jets, fireball/tornado/meteor art) and spot-checked in the daylight arena.
