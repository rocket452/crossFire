# Kage cursor-centered cone slash (revision 287)

The R / touch Slash attack captures the existing cursor/look aim point on activation. Damage uses a circular 3D cone with its apex at Kage's melee origin, centered on that aim point. It replaces the old thin, tilted 180-degree fan. Targets above/below and to either side have equal angular coverage; the complete cone can point straight up or down. Camera movement during an active swing does not redirect its already-applied damage.

Tuning in `ninja-shuriken-config.json`: `slash.coneDegrees=90` (45-degree half-angle), forward depth `range=9m`, origin `height=1.2m`, cooldown 1s, damage 160 with existing Mireguard melee rules, and effect lifetime .24s. The cone radius at its end is depth × tan(half-angle), initially 9m. The obsolete fan angle/thickness settings are removed. A translucent cone shell, rim and guide lines use the same orientation and full depth as the damage volume; the hand sweep follows that frame.

Collision first checks the cursor-centered axis against actual enemy shapes, then tests surface candidates closest to the cone axis/end and apex, covering large colliders whose apex-nearest point lies outside the cone. Each enemy is damaged once per slash regardless of shape count. Buildings still block individual hit traces. Missile and building damage use the same cone; kunai, shuriken, movement and dash are unchanged.

`QA/kage-attacks-test.mjs` checks forward/up/down/vertical aim, eight radial directions, angle/depth rejection, large collider edge intersections, one hit per enemy, intact floor/wall cover, cooldown and reset alongside kunai regression. `QA/kage-aimed-slash-browser.cjs` uses desktop R / touch Slash above an actual Bruiser and confirms damage below, cone geometry/direction and finite weighted pose. Desktop/touch screenshots were inspected. The charged-shuriken regression suite passes.
