# Siege Walker defeat by weapon destruction — revision 241

The Siege Walker dies when all three heavy weapon modules are destroyed: beam cannon (1,800 base durability), rocket pod (1,600), and plasma cannon (1,800). Any of the three can be the final weapon; no additional hull, head or reactor kill is required. The boss bar represents their combined 5,200 base durability and is labelled WEAPONS. The existing temporary `WALKER_WEAPON_HEALTH_SCALE = .1` remains active, so current in-game durability is 180 / 160 / 180 and the boss bar totals 520.

Eye lasers remain an auxiliary attack while the Walker is alive. They have no destructible module, independent visor hit shape, durability, HUD bar or CPU/aim-assist weapon target. Visor/ordinary head hits do not contribute to defeat. Eye attack timing, heavy-weapon/stun gates, death cancellation and reset remain in place.

`WalkerWeapons.health`, `.maxHealth` and `.remaining` expose the totals. `SiegeWalker.modifyDamage` routes incoming combat damage through the existing `damageWeapon`/part-durability logic and returns only the applied weapon damage to `UnitDamage`. The old post-damage callback is removed to avoid double damage. The last module returns the remaining boss health to ensure exact defeat even with fractional damage. Body/hull hits and unlocated global damage do not reduce defeat progress. Existing untagged splash can wear a module only when its impact is beside that module; overkill on one module does not spill into others.

The normal `UnitDamage` zero-health/disable route owns death, so the final break triggers the existing explosion, fall and 18-part smoking physical wreck exactly once. Previously detached weapon pieces are reused. Pending weapon preparations stop through the existing mount/death/arsenal gates; ordnance already launched keeps its existing flight behavior. Reset restores all module durability, attachments, health and attack grace. ARGUS, Bastion and smaller robot health/damage systems are unchanged.

CPU allies aim at surviving weapon centres rather than an invulnerable torso. This uses the existing aim-point hook; player aiming and aim assistance are unchanged. Public tuning remains in `WALKER_WEAPONS`, and Walker diagnostics include `defeatCondition: all-weapons` and `remainingWeapons`.

Death-effect fixtures now destroy the required modules before checking collapse; existing body-only debug kill shortcuts intentionally no longer kill this boss.
