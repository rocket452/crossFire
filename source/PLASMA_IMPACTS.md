# Siege Walker plasma impact effects — revision 220

The Walker's existing plasma artillery now produces a dedicated green/white energy detonation instead of the small generic flash/spark puff. `PlasmaImpactEffects` in `dist/plasma-impact-effects.js` owns a white-hot expanding core, translucent emerald pressure bubble, two staggered ground shockwaves, upward streaks, soft energy discharge and rolling charcoal-green smoke. It uses the artillery's existing colors.

The effect is purely visual. Existing trajectory, warning markers, leading, landing/collision, barrier interception, damage (160 blast power), radius (7m), building impact and hero rescue paths remain authoritative. The two visual ground rings expand to the existing blast radius; they cause no additional damage. Elevated impacts retain the energy/smoke burst without drawing a floating ground ring. Absorbed ice/bubble-barrier shots produce no ground explosion. Pause freezes simulation time; city reset clears all effects. Artillery death does not erase already-landed cosmetic smoke while its existing update continues.

`PLASMA_IMPACT_FX` controls the eight-impact cap, 3.2s maximum lifetime, 0.22s hot flash, 0.8s energy bubble, 28 streaks, eight energy puffs and ten smoke puffs per impact. Five fixed InstancedMesh pools add at most five effect draw calls, regardless of overlap. Excess overlapping impacts replace the oldest visual. No lights, external textures, dependencies, screenshake or camera flashes are added. Each impact uses existing world geometry for depth/occlusion. `plasmaArtillery.impactEffects` diagnostics expose active effects, total impacts, pool counts and draw count.

`QA/plasma-impact-effects-test.mjs` checks the phases, dual rings, smoke tail, surface placement, finite matrices/opacity, fixed pool limits, expiry and reset at 30/60/120Hz and irregular frame steps. `QA/plasma-impact-effects-browser.cjs` checks an actual launched/landed Walker shell, ground detonation, existing blast damage, no damage reapplied by visuals, shader rendering on desktop/touch, lingering smoke, shield interception and reset. Screenshots are inspected. Existing artillery/ballistics/damage/weapon-rotation and civilian route-leading checks pass; their fixtures account for the retained FX root, and the legacy civilian predictor fixture explicitly requests its original immediate spawn and pad coordinates. Physical phone FPS/thermal performance is unmeasured.
