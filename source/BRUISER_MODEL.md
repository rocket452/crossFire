# Bruiser model and headshots — revision 218

`dist/bruiser-model.js` builds original, low-poly articulated armor around the existing Bruiser gameplay anchors. The 1.3 model scale, tread chassis, torso aim pivot, arm aim pivot and muzzle anchor remain. Chamfered chest and shoulder armor, front reactor, rear vents, segmented arms, sensor helmet and a detailed plasma cannon replace the original boxes. Static details merge by articulated parent/material: 2,740 triangles and 30 render meshes including the instanced moving tread/wheel geometry. The cannon has a short cosmetic recoil slide. This requires no new asset downloads or dependencies.

`RAIDER_COMBAT.headshotMultiplier` in `evac-raider.js` is 2. Only a direct hit tagged `head` receives the multiplier through the existing `UnitDamage.hurt`/`modifyDamage` route. Head, torso, chassis and arms have separate oriented hit shapes. The head follows torso and head articulation; the torso's old oversized box no longer absorbs head rays. Body damage is unchanged. Untagged splash/area damage is unchanged; a directly tagged head impact can receive the bonus. Existing immunity/damage guards run before the multiplier and visual effect.

A successful headshot starts a 0.35-second gold-white expanding ring, 18 radial sparks, a white visor flash and a small head recoil. Effects use fixed-size geometry and expire/reset cleanly; they also appear on lethal headshots. The existing red enemy damage flash continues independently. No popup is added. `headshots`, `headshotMultiplier` and `art` diagnostics expose the result. `BRUISER_ART` controls the effect duration, spark count and 0.12-second cannon recoil.

Bruiser HP (1,000), four-shot bursts, 33% independent lead chance, shot speed, movement speed, wave scheduling and hero interactions are unchanged. Aegis/Scythe/Breaker and Mireguards explicitly retain their existing model construction and damage behavior.

Validation: `QA/bruiser-model-headshot-test.mjs` verifies the geometry budget, live rotating hitboxes, exact double head damage, normal body/untagged damage, protection gating, effect/recoil expiry, material isolation, lethal feedback and reset. `QA/bruiser-model-headshot-browser.cjs` exercises the actual arena collision query and shared player damage adapter on desktop/touch: 25 base damage gives 50 head/25 hull, feedback remains independent, death creates five physical wrecks, reset restores the model and evacuation loads all 12 upgraded Bruisers. Screenshots were inspected. Existing burst/intercept/cancellation, Bruiser lifecycle, edge waves, experimental robots, Mireguards, soft separation and Kage smoke checks pass. Physical-phone frame rate/thermal performance remains unmeasured.
