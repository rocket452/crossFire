# Low-health enemy smoke (revision 291)

Smaller robotic enemies emit dark smoke while alive at or below 30% of their own maximum HP, with denser/faster smoke at 15% and below. This covers Bruisers, Aegis, Scythe, Mireguards and enemy repair drones. Siege Walker, Bastion and ARGUS are explicitly excluded from this low-health visual; their existing death explosions and wreck smoke are unchanged.

`ROBOT_LOW_HEALTH_SMOKE` in `dist/robot-death-effects.js` exposes threshold `.3`, critical fraction `.15`, normal interval `.4s`, critical interval `.24s`, 32 active emitters and 96 live-enemy puffs. Smoke follows the reactor/torso/body and emits above the actor’s upper hitboxes so it clears the model, then drifts upward naturally. Live plumes are darker and more opaque than wreck smoke to make the warning readable. Size scales from existing hitboxes. Emission stops on healing above the threshold, death, hiding or despawn; existing puffs fade over the shared 3.2s lifetime. Reset clears all live emitter references and puffs; pause does not create new particles.

Live damage smoke shares the existing 240-puff instanced wreck-smoke pool, material and depth testing. Emission intervals adapt at high enemy counts to preserve the live-puff cap; no extra renderer, texture, light, physics collider or per-enemy draw call is introduced. Smoke does not affect damage, targeting or enemy behavior, and does not render through buildings.

`QA/robot-low-health-smoke-test.mjs` checks threshold boundaries, movement, pause, healing/death/fade/reset, denser critical emissions, caps and a single shared draw at 30/60/120Hz. `QA/robot-low-health-smoke-browser.cjs` applies real damage in the arena to Bruisers, mixed Aegis/Scythe and Mireguards on desktop/touch, verifies cleanup into existing smoking wrecks and excludes low-HP Bastion. Screenshots were inspected. Existing `QA/robot-death-test.mjs` passes explosions, debris physics, bounded smoke and reset regression. Physical phone FPS/thermal performance remains unmeasured.
